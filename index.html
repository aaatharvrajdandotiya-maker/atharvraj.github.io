The Geography of Curiosity

Morena, Madhya Pradesh, is where my perspective was formed. My interest in technology and design comes from a natural curiosity about how things work, how they're built, and how seemingly unrelated ideas can come together. Growing up in a city that wasn't saturated with the tech industry gave me an opportunity to observe technology and design from an unconventional angle.

The Logic and the Language

My work lives at the intersection of web development, artificial intelligence, photography, and digital design. I’m genuinely intrigued by how these fields influence each other. It isn't just about combining code with aesthetics; it’s about exploring how technology, business, and visual expression can be synthesized to build experiences that resonate.

The Observatory

This portfolio is not a collection of finished achievements. It is an observatory—a record of ideas, experiments, discoveries, and connections still taking shape. I am interested in understanding what's possible and finding ways to make those possibilities tangible.
